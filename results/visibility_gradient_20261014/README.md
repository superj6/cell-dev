# Visibility gradient preparation

This is an unreleased study-local adaptation of I's ordinary-mutant measurement. It is prepared for one approved production ABI4 irregular-blink world, two exact evolved founders (D90/L92), two65-row screens on32 shared lives, and selected-mutant/parent confirmation on512 fresh lives. No intermediate rescore is included. Total scope is6,208 research lives plus10 validation lives.

This registered preparation selects irregular blink from the complete X2 information-payoff evidence. Its fixed panels and protocol are prepared once under the root registration; the study remains unreleased until independent review is complete. Both `measure.py` and `monitor.py` default to a no-simulation dry run. The exact extracted founders and historical conditional design are in `../audit_visibility_gradient_20261014/`.

`scoring.py` explicitly uses V4, the unchanged875-value genome, and all58 raw ledger columns. It retains I's production guard and extinction penalties. Low-light captures are an additional diagnostic, without a new fitness reward. Natural deaths and valid guard-ended lives remain in the results. Ordinary cost decomposition uses the kernel's reconstructed column cost; the separately retained accumulated cost is checked with the kernel's rounding tolerance.

The monitor reuses X2's reviewed process ownership, subreaper, teardown and CPU accounting functions unchanged. One two-thread worker performs startup, calibration, screens and confirmation. Startup and both screens have a3,400 CPU-second cumulative ceiling; the whole attempt has a5,400-second ceiling, with ten-second stopping margins. It acknowledges the calibration and screen boundaries after accounting, preserving at least2,000 seconds for confirmation and cleanup. No automatic retry or resume is provided.

The fixed calibration feasibility calculation applies those margins too: measured startup/calibration CPU plus1.5 times projected screening CPU must fit3,390 seconds, and1.5 times projected confirmation CPU must fit1,990 seconds. The four two-seed calibration batches provide rough rates, not an upper bound on unsampled mutants; failure to fit stops the unchanged scope for review.

`numerical_identity()` is called once in the worker: it builds the production libraries and runs exactly two existing ABI3 seed11/T200 compatibility goldens, all charged to startup. The other eight validation lives are actual ABI4 T1024 batches: each parent and its predetermined first mutant on two calibration seeds. Compiler/runtime/libm/Torch/NumPy/source metadata must match the preregistered metadata. Compiled-library hashes and the golden receipt from this sole invocation are explicitly marked newly captured. They are not described as independent replay proof.

Forty-four source/synthetic preparation checks pass without an engine call, compilation or mutant generation; logs and JUnit are under the audit directory.
