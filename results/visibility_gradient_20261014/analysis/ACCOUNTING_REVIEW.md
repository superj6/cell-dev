# Independent gradient row accounting

`offline_accounting.py` is a narrow NumPy-only adaptation of the published study I accountant (`results/decoy_gradient_20261014/offline_accounting.py`: ledger constants, `prices`, `validate_row`, and paired decomposition). It imports no engine, scorer, evaluator, mutation helper or controller. It validates a completed raw row; a study-level reader must separately enforce the protocol, ordered seeds and identity, all required row/index hashes, frozen winners, stage completeness, and monitor budgets before publishing any scientific result.

The production ABI4 ledger has 58 columns. Columns 21/22 remain `beats`/`P_int`; the appended column57 is `low_light_captures`. Every raw ledger column is bound byte-for-byte to its named saved counterpart, except that raw `F` binds to saved `F_kernel`. Named `F` must equal `F_kernel − extinct_charge` exactly. The appended visibility count is integral, nonnegative and at most total captures; it creates no additional reward.

Natural extinction, valid cell/event guards, growth, death and kills remain in every fitness comparison. The independent accountant reconstructs all nine ordinary cost categories in kernel order, guard charge, extinction charge and production fitness. Accumulated event cost uses the kernel's exact tolerance `1e-9 * max(1, abs(reconstructed cost))`; paired fitness decomposition uses reconstructed cost, so legitimate event-summation rounding cannot create an artificial residual. Capacity, ledger mismatch, nonfinite records and unexplained incomplete endings are rejected.

The supplied initial zygote is excluded from the kernel's birth counter. Full-life census is therefore `1 + births − deaths − kills = n_end`. Plane census and cell-time, morphogen balance, click outcomes, receptor counts and visibility counts are checked without assuming a static body. Delivered/dropped spike arrivals and ordinary/wall rejects are disjoint counters and are not compared as subsets. These semantics were checked directly against the production C accountant, including the initial-birth correction found during X2 postanalysis.

The focused tests use synthetic rows containing an ordinary life, a growing life with a kill, a natural extinction and a guarded stop. They check every raw/named column, invalid statuses, rounding tolerance, accounting decomposition and ordered pair seeds. The existing D90 and L92 256-life ABI3 confirmation rows also pass the growth/accounting checks after appending an explicitly synthetic zero visibility column. That is a compatibility check only; it provides no ABI4 blink result. No simulations or compilation were performed.

Confirmed improvements in low-light captures would still not identify memory. Growth, movement, firing, encounter timing and ordinary economics can all contribute. The purpose is to measure accessible local changes in production fitness and capture behavior before considering ordinary evolution in this world.
