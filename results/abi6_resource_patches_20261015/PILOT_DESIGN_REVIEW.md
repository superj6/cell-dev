Source-only recommendation, 2026-10-15. No execution, seed reservation or release. First measure whether inherited
acquisition already obtains persistent food, while a finite external-control panel tests whether stock evidence
can improve acquisition. Do not begin with another evolutionary search or require a hand-built tissue winner.

## What needs new measurement code

Reuse the opaque local-event/action API and physical charging machinery in
`results/delayed_match_information_20261015/prelaunch/policy_sources/{ctl_api.h,ctl_core.c}` as patterns.
Its `raw.py`, `make_ckernel.py` and single-task controller are ABI5-specific, not drop-in ABI6 support.

no realized map, stock, rate, phase, seed, reward, successful-click flag or unmasked confidence. Geometry comes
from permitted pings and own commanded displacement. External central computation remains explicitly free.
Validate the disabled hook against production and script/action replay before interpreting controller scores.

## Interpretation and next decision

Lead with initial-origin and refill-origin harvest, per-patch supply/spill, empty/geometric misses, path, native
income, every physical cost and production F. FIFO provenance quantifies continuing-food income without extra
lives; it is not a source-removal counterfactual. Subtracting refill income holds the observed trajectory fixed
and must be labeled accordingly. Refilled harvest can come from camping; aggregate ledgers do not prove revisits.

Report all population rows and paired life-level contrasts. Useful pursuit plus refill harvest is a foothold;
low performance distinguishes a weak starting point from a world-admission failure. Current-stock information
may legitimately pay before memory or recovery-rate estimation. If competent camping/greedy/patrol saturates
attainable yield, retain that result instead of adding a forced behavior sequence.

These32-seed development comparisons cannot satisfy plan0.4. A subsequent, separately released confirmation
would freeze nine roles—AO, selected C/G/P/allocator, both neutral amplitudes and both do-less derivatives—on
512 fresh paired seeds:4,608 lives. Apply the existing1F/>3SE information and selective-stock contrasts plus
the quarter-gap do-less condition, and require acquisition evidence for an acquisition claim. No memory gate.
Later ordinary evolution should import the full declared population and test its same final endpoint on old
tasks and reserved combinations; this pilot establishes neither adaptation nor a neural mechanism.
