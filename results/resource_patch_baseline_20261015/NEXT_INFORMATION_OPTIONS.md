# Next information measurement: two options

Source-only recommendation, 2026-10-15, written before the inherited baseline outcomes. No controller code,
simulation, build, random draw, seed reservation or new execution release accompanies this document.
The baseline remains 448 scientific plus eight engineering calls, with its fixed endpoints and all population rows.

**Recommendation:** inspect the completed baseline first. If the fixed ancestors obtain food, start with a small
causal removal of stock modulation on those same ancestors. Defer the external multisource estimator and allocator
until the diagnostic identifies a question worth that implementation effort. Neither step requires a handwritten
winning tissue, and the diagnostic alone does not satisfy section 0.4.

This refines the options in [PILOT_DESIGN_REVIEW.md](../resource_patches_20261015/PILOT_DESIGN_REVIEW.md), rather than
committing to its twelve-controller panel before seeing whether inherited pursuit is useful.

|---|---|---|---|

| Stock-modulation ablation on fixed ancestors | The same saved genome develops and acts normally, but a study-local renderer replaces each stock-dependent field amplitude with a constant | The causal whole-life value of the existing sensory modulation for these inherited genotypes | The best available information payoff, a stock estimator or memory mechanism, matched realized tissue, or full world admission |

## Minimal ancestor ablation

Keep AO rank0 and AM reference fixed by the baseline, without promoting a high-scoring population row. Compare:

- **Native:** `a_i(t) = 0.1 + 0.9 * stock_left_i(t) / 2`.
- **Constant full cue:** `a_i(t) = 1`, independent of stock.
- **Constant midpoint cue:** `a_i(t) = 0.55`, independent of stock.

Only the scalar receptor-field calculation changes. Preserve patch positions, identical Gaussian widths, source
periods/phases, capacity, actual depletion, FIFO provenance, harvest rules, prices, and the existing candidate
stream/bound/delay mechanism. Retain ordinary movement, hotspot hysteresis, clicks, local messages and development.
Both neutral arms remain active and receive location-bearing fields at every patch. Fixed source arrivals remain
world events; no clock, map, reward, stock, slot, successful-click flag or source schedule enters the organism.

“Preserve actions” means preserving action rules and available actions. Realized actions must be free to change
causally with sensory input. Consequently stock, admission and spill trajectories can diverge too; forcing the
original actions or stock trajectory would answer a different question. Pair initial world and randomness by
seed, rather than forcing identical receptor acceptances or subsequent cell histories.

Implement exact constants only in an isolated, source-pinned measurement renderer. Native ABI6 requires landmark
less than one: setting it near one merely attenuates stock information and is not an exact neutral arm. The native
renderer must first reproduce production ledgers and full hashes on fixed engineering cases. Source tests should
show that both constant renderers ignore stock and leave all physical source/harvest operations untouched.
No new controller, inference model, action hook or organism input is needed.

The constant-full arm agrees with the native field until the first depletion. The midpoint arm checks sensitivity
to the neutral brightness choice. Neither matches the native event rate along every realized trajectory, so report
receptor counts/charges and all other costs alongside food. Do not restore stock-dependent event rates under an
“equal dose” label or use a realized stock trajectory to choose neutral brightness. Two fixed intensities reduce
ambiguity; they do not completely isolate information from signal strength and confidence effects.

Call this **removal of current-stock modulation**, not complete stock blindness. Own action history, internal
state and physical consequences can still support inference about depletion. A positive contrast can arise from
ordinary attraction toward stocked patches; that is a useful primitive without a learned stock estimate. The same
genome also need not grow the same tissue under altered sensory input, so this is a whole-life intervention rather
than proof of a specific mature circuit or matched-footprint advantage.

The cheapest development comparison can reuse the baseline's 32 fixed reference seeds and 64 native patch rows:
two fixed genomes × two neutral renderers × 32 seeds = **128 new scientific lives**, plus a separately capped
small engineering equivalence check. Reuse is explicit development reuse, never fresh confirmation. Freeze the
two constants and both endpoints before these measurements; keep every result and do not expand the panel to
obtain a favorable gap. A subsequent protocol must bind the original rows, exact renderer sources and all calls.

Report paired life-level mean ± SE for native minus each neutral arm: production F, native F, initial-origin food,
refill-origin food, total income, empty/geometric misses, source/supply/spill/terminal stock and every physical
cost component. Include status/extinction and realized tissue-size differences. Refill food can arise from camping;
the current aggregate ledger does not establish switching, useful revisits, waiting or rate learning. A fitness gap
without increased food may show useful economy; do not relabel it as acquisition improvement.

## When the external instrument earns its implementation cost

If the baseline and ablation leave useful uncollected supply but inherited sensing/action cannot exploit it, a small
external instrument can distinguish available information value from that inherited limitation. Freeze one finite
current-stock value-per-trip allocator, sharing an observation-derived geometry/stock estimator with active greedy
pursuit; retain active camping and patrol. Do not compare an informed greedy controller with itself as the claimed
information gain. No per-patch recovery-rate learning is needed for this first question.

Use only local receptor events and permitted own-action history; no observer truth or confidence feature derived
from hidden stock. Keep physical charging, and explicitly disclose free central computation and preassembled-body
development savings. Match the winning finite reflex's footprint, then include quieter/smaller variants and the
allocator's two constant-renderer ablations. A weak or inactive policy family is a measurement limitation, not a
reason to keep engineering a winner. If a competent greedy or camper already captures most profitable supply,
retain that result rather than forcing visits or changing the world in response to scores.

## Decision after the baseline

| Baseline evidence | Cheapest useful next decision |
|---|---|
| Substantial initial and refill harvest | Run the small fixed-ancestor modulation diagnostic; it directly tests a nearby reusable sensory contribution |
| Initial food, little refill food, substantial uncollected supply | The same ablation can test whether stock cues help or impair existing pursuit; interpret empty/geometric misses and spill before commissioning an allocator |
| Almost no food or severe action/development failure | Inspect existing accounting and limited fixed engineering traces first; use an external instrument only to resolve information availability versus action limitation, without requiring a hand-built tissue winner |
| Strong acquisition and little plausible remaining profitable supply | Consider retention and reserved combinations; do not build a large controller project merely to manufacture a one-F gap |

Positive, null and negative ablation outcomes are all informative. Null does not prove the world lacks usable
information; negative can reveal a mismatch with inherited behavior. The baseline's eight-seed population rows
describe variation and never select the next endpoint. Any later evolution or generalization design must be chosen
from the completed evidence and retain older acquisition checks.

Formal admission still requires [TASK_VARIATION_PLAN.md section 0.4](../../docs/TASK_VARIATION_PLAN.md): at least
512 fresh paired, tuning-disjoint lives; an information advantage of at least 1 F and greater than 3 SE over the best
declared reflex at its footprint; a selective contribution meeting the same threshold; and the best quieter/smaller
reflex gain less than one quarter of the information gap. The ancestor diagnostic does not supply the missing
reflex/do-less comparisons or matched-footprint evidence. Its useful first result is whether an existing local
organism benefits from current depletion cues. Memory-specific payoff is a later question, not an added gate.
