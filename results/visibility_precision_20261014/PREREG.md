# AB prospective allocation comparison

Source-only and unregistered. No AA result selects the founders or policies. This new study does not resume held S,
whose unchanged prerequisites are unsatisfied. Root decides registration and release after independent review.

The question is whether spending the same number of evaluated selection lives on more accurate scoring, at the
cost of fewer generations, gives better fixed endpoints in approved irregular blink. Ranking changes in Z suggest
instability but do not establish rank error or a benefit from more samples. Existing B selected the same winner
under 3 + 4 and 12 + 16; there is no demonstrated precision benefit to assume.

## Fixed searches

Use the exact completed Z_D90 and Z_L92 population rank 0 genomes. The depth policy runs four generations with
3 initial + 4 recheck lives; the precision policy runs one generation with 12 initial + 16 recheck lives.
Keep 32 parents + 224 offspring, recheck the top 64, and select on pooled whole-life production F. Retain sexual
crossover, existing scaled two-point mutation and random ties, with no duplication, deletion or splitting.
No founder protection, best-ever replacement, validation selection, new behavior, intervention, ABI or reward.
Use unchanged `catch_speck_blink20_20_l4`, ABI 4, T1024 and all prices/guard/extinction charges. Low-light captures
remain diagnostic. All parents are reevaluated. Validation and half-life diagnostics do not train organisms.

Four job IDs are D90_depth, D90_precision, L92_depth, L92_precision. Each pair shares one new native run/RNG seed;
the two backgrounds use distinct seeds. Config output directories differ. Prepare one ordinary 32-row initialization
per background after registration, record its operations and RNG state pins, and use the same founder/seed in both
native jobs. Before confirmation, require identical recorded initial fill and first 224 offspring IDs/parents/masks/
point operations. Independent genetic replay must also reproduce all 256 first-candidate raw genomes exactly.
Later trajectories may diverge. No offspring is generated during source-only preparation.

Shared world IDs are intentional within each pair. In generation 0, the depth policy's initial indices 0..2 and
recheck indices 3..6 occur within the precision policy's initial indices 0..11; its recheck indices 12..27 are
additional. Generation 0 validation and final diagnostic IDs also coincide.
The namespace audit must enumerate these actual shared IDs, unique IDs and disjoint 512 confirmation IDs.
This common-random-number comparison is not four independent search replicates. Existing golden seed 11 is the
only deliberate historical extra. No namespaces are selected here.

## Fixed confirmation and outcomes

Freeze all four complete final population rank 0 endpoints together before measuring any confirmation outcome.
Role order is Z_D90, Z_L92, depth_D90, precision_D90, depth_L92, precision_L92, all on the same 512 fresh T1024 seeds.
Primary contrasts: precision_D90−depth_D90 and precision_L92−depth_L92. Secondary: each endpoint minus its own
Z founder. Retain negative/null outcomes and every valid guarded/extinct life with its production penalties.
Decompose F into capture income, ordinary costs, guard charge and extinction charge; report captures, low-light
captures, cells, spikes, clicks and path. Paired life-level SE concerns fixed endpoints, not general policy reliability.

Two background/run pairs cannot establish universal superiority. Equal selection-life count does not equal CPU,
total diagnostic calls or evolutionary opportunity. Better selection may strengthen economy rather than improve
behavior. Capture gains motivate later causal study; low-light captures and F alone do not establish memory or a
more sophisticated algorithm. No AA lesion result gates world use, founder inclusion or this inference.

## Calls and resources

Each depth branch: 4 × (256×3 + 64×4) = 4,096 selection lives, plus 32 validation lives at generations 0 and 3
and six native finals, for 4,134 research calls. Each precision branch: 256×12 + 64×16 = 4,096 selection lives,
plus 16 validation lives at generation 0 and six finals, for 4,118 research calls.

Totals: 16,384 selection + 96 periodic + 24 final + 3,072 confirmation = 19,576 research calls. Each of the four
native searches and the confirmation worker invokes exactly two established ABI 3 seed 11/T200 goldens,
giving 19,586 actual calls. Eight research calls are T512; the other 19,568 are T1024. Native final half lives
reuse the first final seeds. No founder scoring, held-out worlds,
clone conditions, size curve, envelope, duration mixture, new calibration or numerical-identity prepass.

Proposed inclusive cap 9,000 CPU-seconds, with search/startup at most 7,200 and at least 1,800 remaining for confirmation
and teardown. Ten-second stop margins apply. At most two native workers × two threads, then one confirmation × two;
remove elastic thread controls. Include compilation, numerical checks/goldens, monitor/descendants and cleanup.
First attempt only; a failure or cap stop archives incomplete, without dropping a branch, extending or retrying.

Cost review uses all six completed Z confirmation rows, the largest CPU/life within each original/Y/Z lineage,
multiplier 1.5 and 180 CPU-seconds overhead per phase. Search projection: 180 + 1.5 × 8,252 × sum(two lineage maxima).
