Try it: place targets

Pick a body and give it something to chase. The cells below run on the same simulator as the replays, event by event, while you watch.

  • Tap or click an empty spot on the screen to drop a target. Up to six can be out at once.
  • Drag a target to move it. Double-tap it, or tap its ×, to take it away.
  • The body cannot see. A target only makes receptor events more frequent in the bottom-layer cells under its glow. When the centre cell clicks with a target inside the dashed circle, that target is caught.

The playground needs JavaScript to run the simulator.
