Spiking Tissue Atlas

Each organism below grew from one cell. A genome decided how its cells divided and which of up to four cell types each became. Grown cells then react to light where they sit and pass spikes to their neighbours. Nobody wired an input or an output: the score depends only on where in the body the firing ends up.

Press play to watch a body grow, then watch it work through a trial. Switch messages off to see what the cells can do alone. The red overlay marks the cells it cannot do without.
