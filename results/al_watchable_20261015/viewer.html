Mean ± three standard errors, descriptive rather than simultaneous confidence bounds. Paired changes are computed within each shared seed. All results stay visible if the illustration disagrees. A benefit over the other endpoint is not recovery over the common start. A 90% retention diagnostic permits some loss; it is distinct from simultaneous capture gains. One paired search is not a policy reliability estimate. Fitness includes guard and extinction charges; cheaper bodies alone do not establish richer acquisition or memory. POOL differs in several physical settings, so this is not a single-factor mechanism test. These revealed worlds are development cases.
