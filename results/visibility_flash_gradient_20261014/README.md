AC prepares a small ordinary-mutant screen in the approved flash world, followed by paired
confirmation in flash and irregular blink. It reuses both complete Y panels: each original D90/L92
founder and its 64 recorded ordinary mutants. There are no new genetic draws or hand-designed
behaviors.

This directory is currently source-only. No inputs, protocol, registration, compilation, simulation
or release have been created. Preparation later requires root registration and clean terminal
resource clearance after AB. AB's scientific outcome does not choose or gate this study; the
completed Y panels are the scientific dependency.

The screen evaluates all 65 rows per background on 32 shared flash seeds. It selects the highest
mean production-fitness mutant among rows 1–64, breaking ties by the lowest index, even if every
mutant loses to its parent. Both selections freeze before any confirmation. The six roles are the
two originals P, two fixed Y blink selections B, and two new flash selections H. Each role receives
all 512 shared confirmation seeds in each world, including duplicate genomes.

The primary comparisons are H minus P in flash, separately for each background. Within each world,
report H−P, B−P and H−B. Report flash-minus-blink differences for H−P and H−B by subtracting within
the same seed before calculating uncertainty. Capture income, ordinary costs, guard costs and
extinction charges remain separate. These are fixed-background comparisons, not replicated
evolutionary populations or evidence for a memory mechanism.

There are exactly 10,304 research lives: 4,160 screen lives and 6,144 confirmation lives. One
standard numerical-identity call adds two ABI3 compatibility goldens, and flash calibration adds
eight ABI4 lives, for 10,314 total calls. One worker uses two simulation threads. The hard total cap
is 7,200 CPU-seconds, including compilation, startup, calibration, monitor overhead, failures and
teardown. Startup plus calibration has a 180-second ceiling; startup plus screening has a
3,400-second ceiling, preserving 3,800 seconds for confirmation. The monitor stops ten seconds
before each ceiling.

Two fixed cost checks precede expensive work. First, flash calibration and all historical Y blink
screen timings must project the full screen and confirmation inside their allowances. After both
flash winners are frozen, their exact flash and historical blink screen rates must project all
twelve role/world rows inside the confirmation allowance. Failure leaves an incomplete study with
the fixed selections retained; it never causes reselection, dropped roles, a retry or a cap
extension.

`prepare.py`, `measure.py` and `monitor.py` are dry runs by default. A later authorized preparation
uses `prepare.py --prepare`; only a released `monitor.py --run` may launch measurements. The
retained ABI4 scorer and numerical metadata checker are unchanged Y sources. The monitor narrowly
adapts Y's owned-process cleanup and phase acknowledgments. See PREREG.md and SCHEMA.md for exact
contracts.
