# Whole-population development diagnostic

The completed matched selection comparison improves patch/POOL acquisition while losing pursuit capabilities.
This study asks whether the saved populations still contain useful combinations, and how their fixed reporting
endpoints compare with the other survivors. It does not evolve, replace a reporting endpoint, or certify a new winner.

## Fixed scope

Import every ordered row, including duplicates, from the final 32 G8, mean4 and Pareto4 populations. Add the
unchanged AO rank-zero reference as row 96: 97 fixed genomes. Bind each population against its checkpoint and
preserve original IDs, raw float32 bytes and current reporting-genome annotations. No favorable row is filtered
in or out. Materialization follows the completed source revision 5d556443e91d558de8de27ab2e5dc7ae11a51415.

Score all 97 genomes in the exact five worlds from the completed selection study: trained patches, POOL,
persistent pursuit, longer cue gaps and the count-two patch combination. That combination is now revealed
**development evidence**, not an untouched transfer test. Physical prices, local asynchronous rules, ABI6,
life duration T1024 and original extinction accounting remain fixed.

Use one fresh shared panel of 32 life seeds 52510000 through 52510031, subject to collision audit before reservation.
There is no evolutionary RNG. Four startup lives repeat the existing numerical identity and ABI6 truth checks;
engineering seeds are explicitly reused. The complete inventory is **97 × 5 × 32 = 15,520 research + 4 startup
= 15,524 calls**. Store 20 raw cohort/world batches and 20 scored batches, with explicit flattened row/seed order;
these contain 485 genome/world records. Returned raw bytes precede validation. Invalid rows stop inference;
ordinary guards/extinction retain their prescribed charges and are not selectively dropped.

## Readouts and limits

Report all per-row capture, replenished-food, physical-cost and production-F means/paired SE over the 32 whole
life seeds, including paired row-minus-AO results. Population means average the 32 ordered genomes within each
shared seed before computing uncertainty; genomes are not treated as independent life replicates. Report
capture/F distributions and where the unchanged historical reporting endpoint lies within each population.

For a descriptive joint-acquisition ordering, reuse the three fixed AO scales 19.046875, 16.029296875, 5.919921875.
Take the minimum of patch/POOL/persistent capture fractions, then ordinary F with the original 50/25/25 weights,
then native ID ascending. Blink and the revealed patch combination remain additional diagnostics. Do not attach
a naive linear SE to this minimum or turn its maximum into a confirmed winner.

For mean4 and Pareto4, compare cached final 16-life objectives with the new 32-life measurements using exact
survivor-ID mappings. The cached sample has 8 patch, 4 POOL and 4 persistent lives. G8 lacks cached per-domain
capture arrays, so only its fresh ordering is available. Ranking disagreement is descriptive; it does not by
itself prove that scoring noise caused the completed strategy difference. Label all 32-life bounds and any
90%-AO capture margins exploratory, with multiplicity and selection optimism explicit.

One small panel can reveal useful variation and guide the next experiment. It cannot prove a useful combination
absent from the population, certify the best of 96 rows, or establish a new capability. Any later prospectively
chosen endpoint requires fresh confirmation. No repeat panel, top-up, retrospective endpoint substitution or
mutation is part of this study. Reconsider the highest-value next step after the complete panel.
