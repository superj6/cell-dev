# AG independent source draft

The protocol hash is deliberately unset. This reader cannot emit AG outcomes
before registration, reviewed preparation and separate runtime release.
No AG inputs, candidate genotypes, stage IDs, compiled libraries or lives were
created by this reader work.

The implementation reuses AE's one-world lifecycle and the unchanged AC ABI4
whole-life accountant. It retains all 58 raw ledger columns, all 61 named
production columns, natural extinction and paid guards. Initial cells are not
births. Column 57 is low_light_captures; bright captures are captures minus that
counter. The two H-minus-own-P contrasts each contain 66 paired vectors,
including the four additive fitness terms, for 132 vectors total.

The complete AD production world is bound by its archived protocol and an
independent reconstruction of all 70 packed coordinates. The original Y panels
and their recorded mutations remain the candidate source. All 130 AC dim-flash
row timings are verified against their original identities, indices and completed
summary; they are cost floors, not fresh dark-versus-dim comparisons. The
current calibration and selected-role feasibility rules remain the reviewed AE
rules. A clean terminal AF is a resource prerequisite only; its outcome sign
and scientific completeness do not choose candidates or gate the world.

The 19 focused checks use synthetic four-role rows in memory and existing
completed source/timing evidence. No fixture tree was generated. They cover
all 132 paired vectors against the actual producer's pure report function,
low-light/census/fitness/role corruption, the exact AD world/packing, all 130
historical timing identities, corrupted timing rejection and unset-protocol
refusal. The earlier failed check was a mistyped expected accountant checksum;
the retained accountant itself was correct and unchanged. The failed log and
initial test source are preserved.

Later panel replay checks recorded additions only, with no new random draws or
life calls. Confirmation uncertainty concerns these fixed genomes and world
seeds. Low-light captures and fitness gains do not, by themselves, identify
memory, prediction or more sophisticated computation.
