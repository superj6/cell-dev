# Conditional next-study choices

Written without reading current outcomes, curves or checkpoints. This is a decision note, not a new protocol
or release. Generation8 remains primary; generation4 cannot replace it.

Judge acquisition by total/refill food alongside F and costs. An unresolved90% margin is not proven forgetting;
passing it still permits some loss. Training-only gain is useful specialization, not established general reuse.

| Fixed endpoint evidence | Highest-value next investment | Why this fits cumulative reuse |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition plus retention; transfer encouraging | **Extend depth unchanged**, from the full final population, with a fixed new terminal endpoint and the same direct capability checks. | Let profitable primitives combine. AO added acquisition after generation8. A longer trajectory does not establish search reliability. |
| Acquisition with resolved forgetting | **One matched allocation comparison:** current mix versus modestly more exposure to the lost capability, same final population and prices. | AL/AO show exposure can recover or preserve acquisition, with tradeoffs. Allocation changes both objective weight and scoring precision; do not claim otherwise. |
| Economy only | **Extend unchanged if food is retained.** If the completed trajectory consistently trades food for savings, prefer one focused incentive comparison. | Cheaper capability is useful but not added acquisition. Identify the dominant actual charge first. AM's fourfold reward increased body cost without joint superiority; avoid a price sweep. Repricing alone cannot predict what evolves. |
| No resolved gain | **One independent short replication**, preserving founders, world, mixture and prices. Prefer depth instead if the fixed midpoint-to-final comparison clearly suggests late acquisition. | One short search cannot identify a systematic accessibility problem. Do not immediately change the environment or infer a memory/substrate ceiling. |

If training acquisition/retention improve but transfer does not, consider a small fixed family of physical
geometries/rates and reserve a new combination. The revealed combination becomes development data. Keep food
rewards ordinary; prescribe no visit order and no population-responsive difficulty.

Best cheap causal follow-up: apply the **existing two constant-brightness renderers to start and descendant**.
Compare the evolutionary capture gain under native versus constant rendering, testing whether added performance
depends on stock modulation. This whole-life interaction still mixes development and sensory-dose effects;
it identifies neither memory nor a mature circuit. A broad freeze that changes firing is less diagnostic.
Use the probe only when it can change the next decision; mechanism completeness is not a prerequisite to progress.
