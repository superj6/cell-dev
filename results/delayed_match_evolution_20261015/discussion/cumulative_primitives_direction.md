The concrete nearby addition is **three persistent, depletable food patches with locally visible stock**. Keep the existing scalar spatial receptors, movement, hotspot click and physical prices. Initially stocked patches use the familiar Gaussian attraction geometry and remain at fixed world positions after collection. A successful click removes one available packet and earns the ordinary food income; no stock means no food. Each patch replenishes under a bounded, fixed physical law. Its local field changes with available stock; faint persistent landmarks can identify depleted locations without revealing their productivity. Assign replenishment rates independently of appearance and position at life start. Use a fixed distribution of reachable layouts and enough time for repeated harvest/revisit cycles after development. Exact stock, recovery and travel scales need a concrete world/ABI contract; none are registered here.

This creates several potentially profitable partial strategies under one unchanged objective: acquire nearby food, reduce empty clicking, wait at a recovering patch, leave depleted stock for another patch, revisit productive places, or allocate effort using accumulated evidence. Each pays only through food obtained minus physical costs. There are no bonuses for “remember,” “inspect,” or a named sequence, and no requirement to perform all these behaviors. A simple reactive stock follower or fixed patrol may succeed; they remain legitimate comparisons. Profitable camping is compatible with a foothold, but if it captures nearly all available value, the proposed ecology offers little additional information payoff. Check that tradeoff prospectively with stock/flow accounting and competent physical controls; freeze the resulting laws and instance distribution before evolution. Do not adjust difficulty to population progress.
