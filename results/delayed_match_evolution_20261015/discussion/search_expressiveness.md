The source does not establish a capacity ceiling for this substrate. It establishes a small shared developmental program, restricted local communication and action interfaces, and several search biases that could make useful combinations difficult to discover or preserve. The strongest next measurements would separate inaccessible expression, destructive variation and unreliable selection before attributing failure to what cells can represent. This note reads current source, frozen starting inputs and completed studies only. No active outcomes, new lives, builds, genetic draws or frozen-file edits were used.

First, this is an **875-parameter genome, not 875 genes**. The ABI provides 16 regulatory gene slots. The exact inherited [population](../inputs/input_population.json), SHA256 `56f81eaf0418a6589cca6505d33215c3e1448893d5285b7e909875217a7a6d19`, contains 32 genomes, each with only slots 0–7 active. Every genome has the same decoded update-rate vector:

`[1/32, 1/16, 1/16, 1/2, 1/2, 1/32, 1/16, 1/4]` per own tick.

Across the 256 active slot occurrences, these are 64 at 1/32, 96 at 1/16, 32 at 1/4 and 64 at 1/2. All eight active regulatory rows in every inherited genome have nonzero receptor-trace coefficients `W[k,22]`. These are static coefficient/rate facts, not evidence that a cue changes useful behavior. The frozen structural-off search cannot activate the eight spare slots: point mutation excludes `act`, duplication/deletion are off, and both crossover parents begin with the same active mask. Thus current search uses eight active genes throughout, even though the source supports sixteen. [Genome operators](../../../src/genome.py), [frozen configuration](../configs/mixed.yaml).

Two details sharpen these distinctions. With eight active genes, only **379 of the 875 raw entries are point-mutable**: 172 regulatory and 207 phenotype entries. The two changes cannot directly supply one new regulatory coefficient and one new readout coefficient in the same mutation step, although successive generations and crossover can combine them. Before decoding, the 23 phenotype values lie in an affine space of dimension at most eight for a fixed genome; this is a real coupling of outputs, not a count of possible decoded phenotypes. Nonlinear recurrence and quantization still permit many states. [Layout and masks](../../../src/genome.py), [decoding](../../../src/life/spec.py).

The same program must develop the body from one cell and operate it; it has no separate adult training phase. Growth, maintenance and a larger physical footprint have costs, while T85 gives little time to repay developmental investment. This can favor accessible early behaviors without proving that elaborate behavior is unrepresentable. The sparse fabric is laterally unbounded; the legacy `lattice.radius: 9` config is not the life-mode body boundary. The current world instead has explicit 1,024-cell and 5,000,000-event execution guards, plus four height planes. Those are real run limits, not evidence that useful computation has reached them.

For a fixed gene target with no feedback, a rate of 1/32 gives an e-fold relaxation of about 31.5 own ticks, roughly 63 world-time units near the nominal tick period of 2. That is only an isolated-relaxation example, not the memory lifetime of the recurrent system. A raw trace with time constant 16 retains about 29% after 20 units and 8% after 40 units; it does not vanish at 16. Current cells can feed those traces into gene state. Whether the inherited dynamics write a discriminable cue, preserve it and route it to the clicker is the open question. Cells have local dynamics and asynchronous ticks, not a readable organism clock. [Cell update and receive rules](../../../src/life/kernel.c).

Completed evidence cautions against premature redesign. Ordinary continuations already accumulated capture-funded gains; AO's unchanged branch improved after generation 8. Earlier structural searches and final copied-gene knockouts did not demonstrate useful genome expansion, but the connectivity audit found transient paths from copied genes to phenotype readouts. Those nulls do not demonstrate exhausted capacity. The older D/G/L selection audit found 36.5% parent-slot replacement after recheck under **3+4**, not today's 8+8; it shows consequential sampling, not its present magnitude or the benefit of a larger budget. Earlier frozen-tissue/hidden-message effects establish communication contributions without identifying a reusable learning algorithm. The planned R weight-refresh study produced zero measurement lives after a dependency stop, so it supplies no positive plasticity result. I recommend choosing among these three bounded measurements rather than treating them as a prerequisite ladder:

1. **Decode accessibility from saved genomes, with zero lives.** For the inherited population and a fixed set of recorded completed-AO offspring, reconstruct pre/post-crossover and pre/post-mutation values using recorded operations. Count exact genotype novelty, zygote phenotype changes, clipping and decoded rate-class changes. For each affine readout, bound its range over active gene states in `[0,1]`: an interval entirely inside one decode bin proves that readout cannot respond to those gene states; a broad interval proves only potential, not reachable behavior. This distinguishes genuinely ineffective coefficient routes from a merely small gene count. Do not equate unchanged zygote phenotype with whole-life neutrality. The existing copied-gene analytical work provides a precedent; no random mutation panel is needed.

2. **Separate crossover from point-mutation effects in a small fixed family panel.** Predetermine four recorded completed-AO parent pairs/offspring by generation and ID, independent of outcome. Evaluate five variants per family: parent A, parent B, recorded crossover alone, that crossover with its recorded mutations, and parent A with the same point additions. All inherited slots are active in the same positions, so the last comparison is well-defined after checking the recorded indices. At 64 shared fresh lives in each of POOL and persistent, this is **2,560 diagnostic lives**, plus explicitly counted startup checks if selected. Compare acquisition and physical costs, not just net F. It would reveal whether these particular recombinations destroy useful behavior, whether mutation causes the loss, or whether neither does. Four families and 64 lives are exploratory, not a general operator-policy verdict or world-admission claim; no new genetic draws or cell-rule changes are required.

3. **Measure ranking reliability separately from what is rewarded, with saved data.** Use the completed AO fixed-genome confirmation rows to form predetermined disjoint 8+8 packets under its recorded balanced allocation; compare packet rankings against the full saved panel means and report capture/income/cost decompositions. This is a fixed-panel diagnostic of sampling sensitivity, not a reconstruction of selection among all 256 candidates or proof that more rechecks improve evolution. Once the current study is terminal and released, the same predeclared analysis can use its already-collected rows for the actual 4/2/2 mixture and class-conditioned matching outcomes. No active rows are read now, no new lives are needed, and no endpoint may be reselected. Stable net-F rankings accompanied by loss of conditional response suggest an objective tradeoff; unstable rankings suggest a precision issue worth testing separately.

Of these, the first is the cheapest direct check of expression restrictions; the family panel gives the clearest causal operator comparison if that is the next uncertainty worth resolving. None establishes generality beyond practical handwritten programs. That objective requires useful reusable behavior on unfamiliar interaction structures, with one fixed genome, substantial comparisons and clearly separated tuning/evaluation contexts. The present source leaves room for that investigation without promising it or requiring redesigned cells.
