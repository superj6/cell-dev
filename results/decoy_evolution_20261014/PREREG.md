# U: ordinary evolution with useful and harmful light sources

U asks whether ordinary evolution from both fixed I winners makes further progress in the existing harmful-decoy
world, and whether that progress depends on the spatial distinction between the two light sources or on lateral
messages. This is a bounded exploratory study, with all outcomes retained.

Two new populations start from I s5 row32 and s7 row1, respectively. Each contains its exact founder plus31
ordinary two-point-mutated copies. Search seeds53091/53092 each run4 generations of ordinary32+224 sexual
evolution:3 common lives, then4 additional common lives for the64-candidate shortlist. There is no duplication,
deletion, protected founder or best-ever endpoint. The final population's rank0 is the endpoint even if worse.

Selection uses exactly F/I's ABI3 harmful-decoy world: T1024, d0=1, d_min=.5, sigma=.6, lam_s=2, b2_amp=1,
b2_sigma=1.2, b2_value=-50, b2_p=1, b2_mode=1; all remaining physics/prices are unchanged. The world is fixed
independently of the population. Behavior remains evolved, local and asynchronous.

Both generation4 rank0 endpoints must be complete, valid and frozen before any fresh confirmation. Four roles
(both I founders and both endpoints) share512 fresh block106 seeds52,060,000..52,060,511 in four conditions:

1. `trained`: the exact selection world, whole-life accounting.
2. `width_swapped`: sigma=1.2 and b2_sigma=.6; identities, rewards, all other settings and whole-life accounting stay fixed.
3. `frozen`: trained world with freeze at512, accounting from544 to1024.
4. `frozen_lateral_off`: the identical freeze and accounting window, also lateral_off at512.

Primary outcomes are each endpoint minus its own founder's trained-world F, with mean and SE over512 paired seeds.
Secondary interactions subtract the paired endpoint-founder effect in width_swapped from that in trained, and
subtract frozen_lateral_off from frozen. Report both signs, every arm's absolute outcomes, captures, poison
clicks, total clicks, missed clicks, cell counts, movement, spikes/messages, capture income, poison/miss/ordinary
costs, and guard/extinction charges. Each contrast is formed within seed before estimating SE. A3-SE or+.5F flag
is descriptive, with no multiplicity-adjusted discovery claim or automatic admission decision.

Improved F can arise from doing less. Capture, poison and complete cost accounting therefore accompany every
fitness contrast. Width reversal changes the spatial distribution of receptor signals as well as their useful
association: sensitivity to that reversal does not by itself prove discrimination, memory, learning or a new
algorithm. Freeze bundles growth and regulatory/phenotypic updates; the frozen lateral contrast tests a specific
message contribution in that altered regime, without identifying a circuit or proving mature anatomy.

The hard allocation is7200 local CPU-seconds, with at most4simulation threads during the two searches and2
during confirmation. Aggregate search-stage CPU is capped at3600, reserving at least3600 of the total for
confirmation and shutdown. Search cannot borrow this reserve. A stopped search or missing arm leaves U
incomplete; there is no endpoint substitution, rescue, automatic restart, extension or partial-study inference.

Research accounting:8192 selection lives +64 periodic validation lives (g0/g3) +12 tiny final lives +8192 fresh
confirmation lives =16460. Of the tiny final lives,4 last T512 and deliberately reuse the full-life final0 seeds;
the others last T1024. There are128 unique hashed search/diagnostic IDs. Periodic/tiny diagnostics do not select
the registered endpoints. Six existing T200 startup golden lives (full+fast in each search process and the
confirmation process) are separate validation within the same CPU cap. Preparation and tests simulate zero lives.

Source/numerical identities, input archives, exact initial populations, prospective namespaces and all genotype
selection rules are pinned before release. Capacity failures or ledger mismatches block inference; legitimate
guards/extinction retain production penalties. Two selected founder lineages cannot estimate general search
reliability, and life-level uncertainty is not evolutionary replicate uncertainty.
