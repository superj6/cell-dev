# AI: independent repetition of the two-slot exploratory reserve

This is a source-only candidate. No registration, scientific inputs, initial populations, libraries, or lives have been produced. Later preparation and release require root authorization. A separate prepared-status receipt will describe any authorized change in that state; this historical source-only text stays pinned.

The experiment repeats AH's matched four-generation control/reserve comparison with an independent native seed and fresh confirmation lives. AH's relative capture advantage motivates this replication; the decision to repeat was made after that result. The founder remains AE L92 row 31, a deliberately selected case originally motivated by AE's capture gain and AF's later loss. Both AI arms are new searches, and neither AH nor AF is reused as their control. The ordinary fitness function, prices, ABI3 world, organism rules, and production source remain unchanged.

In generations 0 through 2, the reserve arm chooses two distinct members uniformly from all 224 fresh offspring. Those two enter the 64-member recheck shortlist and survive into the 32-member next population. The remaining places follow the ordinary fitness rankings, excluding the protected members. Protection expires after that selection. Generation 3 uses ordinary selection in both arms. All selected survivors retain pooled-fitness rank order, including the rank-zero organism used by final diagnostics.

The separate adapter consumes no genetic or tie RNG draws. A stateless SHA256 rejection lottery derives its two choices from the run seed and generation. Its domain deliberately remains `cell-dev:AH:reserve:v1`, and the adapter and search driver are byte-identical to AH. Both arms share the new native genetic seed, initial cohort, first offspring, and life seed schedule intentionally. They consume the same three native tie-permutation calls per generation. Subsequent genomes and actual CPU costs may differ.

`search.py` wraps the unchanged native runner. The adapter and its policy are bound in both configuration and checkpoint numerical identity. The original numerical identity runs once per worker. Full ranking inputs, orders, state hashes, and protected IDs are embedded in the existing durable lineage log. The scientific pilot permits no resume; synthetic tests cover state restoration without any engine lives.

The scope is 8,268 native research lives plus 1,536 confirmation lives, with six standard golden lives: 9,810 total calls. Search uses two workers with two threads each. Confirmation uses one worker with two threads. The inclusive CPU cap is 5,400 seconds, with a 3,400-second search ceiling and 2,000-second confirmation reserve. The monitor stops at 3,390 and 5,390 seconds to leave cleanup room. Historical timing projections are feasibility checks, not cost bounds for new offspring.

The primary contrast is reserve endpoint minus control endpoint captures. Production fitness, capture income, ordinary costs, guard charges, extinction charges, and both endpoint-minus-start gains are also retained. Better fitness through lower costs alone is not evidence of improved acquisition. Report AI independently of AH. The 512 confirmation lives measure each fixed endpoint pair; they are not 512 independent searches. Even two matched search pairs on this one deliberately chosen background cannot establish broad policy reliability, a memory mechanism, or necessary low-fitness stepping stones.

Preparation requires completed AE input provenance and clean AH terminal resource clearance. AH's observed result motivates the replication; the operational dependency checks its clean terminal state. A missing arm, cap stop, incomplete confirmation, or failed audit yields an incomplete study with no complete-study outcome claim. There is no retry, endpoint replacement, scope reduction, or budget extension.

`prepare.py` and `measure.py` default to dry-run reports. `run_budgeted.py` requires explicit root release and `--run`. `SCHEMA.md` defines the registration, raw, checkpoint, and resource contracts; `PREREG.md` fixes the comparisons and interpretation.
