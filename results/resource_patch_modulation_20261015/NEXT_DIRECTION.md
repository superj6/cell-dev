# Next investment after stock modulation

Recommendation: prioritize one small ordinary continuation of the **full AO balanced population**, with fixed
patch/POOL/persistent exposure and unchanged prices. The completed baseline already establishes profitable
inherited pursuit and replenished-food collection. The modulation result now adds a useful causal foothold:
AO native minus constant0.55 gains **5.25 ±0.91 captures**, including **4.41 ±0.86 replenished packets**, and
**2.924 ±0.456 F**; native also beats constant1, and AM has the same direction. These are paired development
results from fixed genomes, not new evolution or a stock-estimation algorithm. The lower empty-click count is
consistent with useful depletion-sensitive attraction; tissue development and signal-strength effects remain.

Make the next scientific question **whether this inherited foothold supports added acquisition without losing
older acquisition**, rather than whether we can implement a central allocator. A concrete nearby design is one
eight-generation continuation, fixed 4/2/2 patch/POOL/persistent evaluation allocation, original physical prices,
and the final rank-zero genome as the sole primary endpoint. Do not promote a diagnostic population row or a
favorable midpoint. Compare that same unchanged endpoint with its frozen start on the three familiar worlds and
one predeclared, selection-excluded combination of patch geometry and replenishment periods. Keep replenished
food, total captures, physical costs and retention alongside F. A quiet or smaller winner is a valid outcome,
but economy alone is not added acquisition or cumulative capability. Exact worlds, native configuration, fresh
seed blocks and an affordable call/CPU cap require a separate prospective design; this note launches nothing.

There is an explicit planning issue: [section0.4](../../docs/TASK_VARIATION_PLAN.md#04-the-admission-rule-replaces-every-positive-control-must-pass-gate)
currently requires **512 fresh paired lives**, an information gap and selective contribution each at least1F and
greater than3SE, and the best quieter/smaller reflex gain below one-quarter of that gap before a world enters
selection. This diagnostic supplies none of the missing strongest-active-reflex, matched-footprint or do-less
comparisons, and its reused32 lives cannot satisfy that rule. Do not call it formal admission.

I recommend an explicit narrow plan revision separating **bounded exploratory selection** from **formal world
admission**: permit the proposed short continuation because an inherited organism already acquires continuing
resources and benefits from the state-dependent field, while keeping all section0.4 criteria for an admission
claim. This avoids making a substantial new external-controller project a prerequisite for testing evolutionary
accessibility. Record that change before a run; do not silently waive the existing rule. If the rule remains literal,
the next admissible selection step instead needs a finite camping/greedy/patrol and do-less payoff comparison;
keep that instrument small and do not add recovery-history learning merely to manufacture complexity.

Had modulation been null or negative, I would first use its saved cost, miss, spill and food decomposition to
decide whether a nearby acquisition opportunity remained. A null in inherited pursuit would not establish that
stock information is worthless or justify changing prices/worlds until a gap appears. The cheap alternative would
be one bounded accessibility pilot under the same explicitly revised exploratory scope, with acquisition and
retention as its decision criteria—not a growing controller tournament or a memory-specific admission demand.
The actual positive result makes that continuation the stronger next investment.
