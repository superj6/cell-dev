Interpretation limits:

- Confirmed loss measures one duplicated-origin gene's whole-life contribution, including development.
- A duplicate may replace an old gene or share a redundant role; this does not prove expanded computation.
- The knockout acts from birth in every cell; it does not isolate an adult or fixed-tissue mechanism.
- Individual null effects do not establish zero function or exclude redundancy and epistasis.
- Initial strongest-gene selection is followed by fresh confirmation, retaining both signs.
- Three-SE and greater-than0.5F confirmation flags are descriptive, not a20-run G-use criterion.
- Two fixed endpoints do not establish general mutation-policy superiority.
- Independent replay reconstructs recorded genetics and final rank0, not RNG draws or selection.
- Checkpoint byte hashes and the original pinned checkpoint-validation receipt are retained; this NumPy analysis does not independently deserialize checkpoint objects.
