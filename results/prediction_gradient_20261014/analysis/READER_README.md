AE reader preparation is source-only and unregistered. `analyze.py` has no protocol
anchor and emits no scientific results until a separately reviewed protocol is
bound. No retained candidate inputs have been copied and no actual panel replay,
compilation or simulation has been performed here.

The reader adapts the frozen AC reader and Y/AC whole-life accountant. New rows
have 57 ABI3 ledger columns and 60 saved scoring columns; no low-light column is
invented. Initial cells are excluded from births, so the census uses
`1 + births - deaths - kills`. Natural extinction and valid paid guards remain;
fitness is kernel fitness minus the production extinction charge.

A complete study requires 130 screen rows, four two-life calibration rows and
four 512-life confirmation rows: 138 raw rows and 6,216 raw lives. One recorded
standard identity invocation adds two ABI3 compatibility goldens, for 6,218 calls.
The four fixed roles are P_D90, P_L92, H_D90 and H_L92. Each winner is selected from
all 64 retained mutants by its whole 32-life mean, with the lowest row breaking
ties, even if it loses to its parent. Both own-parent contrasts retain 64 metrics
and 512 paired values: 128 output vectors. Capture income and ordinary, guard and
extinction charges are separate signed components of the fitness gain.

Protocol checks reconstruct all 66 packed V world fields from pinned source AST,
verify every historical Y timing row and all original panel hashes, and require
recorded-operation replay of all 130 panel rows with the retained helper. The
replay imports only pinned genome/layout definitions and does not generate random
draws. Derived configurations may change only the declared world, ABI and exact
V overrides. V and Y both use d0=1 and d_min=0.5.

Resource and phase checks reuse AC's actual four-field process receipts, immutable
phase snapshots, first-attempt accounting and standard identity/library checks.
The changed cost gates use all 130 historical row rates, including the slow D90
row 45. Each screen row has its own historical floor; the unknown selected mutant
must fit the lineage's historical maximum before screening. After selection, each
of the four roles uses its own observed/historical maximum. These estimates do not
bound fresh-life costs; the monitor's 5,400/3,400/180 CPU-second caps remain final.

The 38-check suite uses one shared 2.5 MB synthetic archive, restoring corruption
cases in place. It exercises actual producer cost and report function ASTs. A
first test-only count incorrectly included three row-index files; its failure log
is retained. After correcting the NPZ filter all 38 checks passed. Eight existing
metadata/path checks were rerun after a portability-only test-path adjustment.
Actual registered-input and recorded-panel replay checks remain pending.

Two mutation neighborhoods are not independent evolutionary replicates. Capture
gains would establish local accessibility for these fixed genomes, not tracking,
prediction, memory, or a general search guarantee. Historical Y fitness is not a
paired comparison between worlds. Negative and unresolved results remain results.
