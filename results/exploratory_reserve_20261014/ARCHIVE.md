This snapshot contains the exact registered inputs, shared initial population,
source, independent reader and review evidence for one matched exploratory
reserve pilot. It contains no AH simulations, compiled study libraries or
scientific outcomes. Publication does not release the monitor.

Both four-generation arms start from the same AE L92 row 31 and shared 32-row
initial population in the exact persistent-target ABI3 world. The reserve arm
protects two uniformly chosen fresh offspring during its first three selection
rounds; its final round and the entire control use ordinary selection. Fitness,
prices and local asynchronous organism rules are unchanged. The primary
comparison is captures by the two fixed endpoints on 512 fresh shared seeds.
The starting genotype is also retained as a confirmation role. The planned
scope is 9,804 research calls and six existing numerical identity golden calls,
within 5,400 CPU seconds inclusive. This deliberately selected single L92 case
cannot establish general policy reliability or a memory mechanism.

`manifest.json` maps every file to its original path, SHA256 and size.
`prelaunch/manifest.json` preserves this preparation snapshot. Original README,
preregistration and source-review wording is retained with its historical date
and scope; `PREPARED_STATUS.json` and the bound analysis receipts identify the
actual prepared state. Preserved checker and test failures are documented
history, not silently replaced evidence.

Scientific inputs and the definitions needed for offline initialization replay
are inside this folder: study-local paths, `production/` for production sources,
and `original/` for dependency receipts. Offline preparation verification can
check the exact 32 initial rows by replaying 62 recorded additions without new
random draws or a simulation. Full outcome analysis refuses the missing ready
and runtime evidence.

inventories are not duplicated here. This distinction does not omit current
