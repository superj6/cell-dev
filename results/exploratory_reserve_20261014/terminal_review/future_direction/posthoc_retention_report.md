This does not support a protected-genetic stepping-stone explanation for the winning endpoint. Selection displacement, rank ordering, and different evaluation allocation can redirect the later search even when protected lineages disappear. The paired search has a positive realized endpoint effect, but this single trajectory does not identify which indirect route mattered or show that the reserve is reliably helpful. The recount uses parental reachability only; it does not assign functional contribution to inherited units or reconstruct unmeasured counterfactual trajectories.

The deferred ordinary-depth design remains useful, particularly if replication is negative or inconsistent. Starting from all 32 retained AF L92 population rows is stronger for that question than remutating only rank zero. Replication now addresses the most immediate uncertainty with fewer changes; neither another tiny policy adjustment nor a mutation-operator experiment is justified by this first positive result alone.
