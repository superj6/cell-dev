# Follow-up judgment after the bounded continuation

Retain the full final population and frozen reporting genome ID 1415 as evidence and potential starting
material. The current result establishes gains in two older acquisition tasks relative to the fixed Pareto4
comparator and clears the registered 90% older-world retention diagnostics. It does not establish better
resource-patch acquisition, better reserved-layout performance, or joint superiority over G8; the revealed
layout has a resolved loss against the fixed G8 comparator. Historical endpoints remain immutable.

This outcome gives no reason to declare the substrate exhausted. It also does not justify attributing the
improvement to denser evaluation, automatically extending the same search, or changing the number of genes
on the assumption that capacity is the limiting factor. This pilot jointly changed depth and sampling, and
only one evolutionary trajectory was run. Preserved genetic diversity and functional capacity are different
questions; larger bodies and increased maintenance expenditure do not establish richer computation.

The next high-value direction is to connect an accessible, richer interaction to measurable acquisition
headroom while keeping the recovered older abilities as fixed reference tasks. Before a larger continuation
or substrate change, use a bounded, registered comparison to measure task accessibility and the acquisition
value of sensory information. A null result would not identify a representation ceiling. An unchanged-law
mechanism/readout comparison can help identify which local signals and actions affect acquisition; a capacity
or operator comparison needs its own matched control and must not infer a ceiling from this endpoint alone.

For a new environment, preserve the cumulative-primitives requirement: ordinary existing pursuit must still
pay immediately; partial progress should earn food; added state/history or composition should create a
measurable advantage over the declared active alternatives. Reserve genuinely new cases before selection,
keep physical laws and prices fixed, and evaluate one frozen genome across them. No handwritten winning
body or prescribed behavioral sequence should become an admission gate. Current-stock sensitivity can be
useful without a memory-specific claim.

A concrete design candidate is **contact-created food that becomes collectible later**: contact with a site
creates a finite future resource under fixed world laws, while ordinary immediately collectible food remains
available and readiness is locally visible. This could combine existing pursuit and depletion sensing with
consequences of earlier actions. Camping, greedy collection, patrol and revisits should all remain legitimate
partial strategies. Its information payoff and travel/recovery economics need a bounded comparison before
assuming that it rewards a richer solution. Neither a memory-specific gap nor a fully explained circuit
should become a prerequisite, and no controller or environment implementation is launched here.

A longer continuation remains an option if a specific acquisition headroom or mechanism hypothesis warrants
its cost. Repeating selection tweaks without such a hypothesis is not the default recommendation. No follow-up
study, extra life, source mutation or endpoint substitution is initiated by this decision note. Standing
local-work authorization is unchanged; a future experiment still needs a concrete fixed protocol and budget.
