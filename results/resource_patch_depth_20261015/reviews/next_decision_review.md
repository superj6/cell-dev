# Independent scientific review of the follow-up decision

No material disagreement. This review reads completed saved evidence only; it executes no simulator, build,
random draw, new statistical test or study. Arithmetic and artifact auditing are handled separately.

The recommendation correctly recognizes substantive acquisition recovery: relative to the fixed Pareto4
comparator, POOL gains 0.812500 ± 0.223576 captures and persistent pursuit gains 0.849609 ± 0.144374.
Both gains survive higher costs. All nine registered older-world 90% retention margins clear three SE;
this is useful retention evidence, permitting some loss, not lossless preservation or superiority over all
founding genomes. Patch/refill and reserved-layout improvements remain unestablished. The revealed-layout
loss against G8 (−0.982422 ± 0.233320 captures) limits cumulative-generality claims. One combined depth/
precision trajectory cannot establish which intervention caused progress or how reliably it recurs.

A more concrete next design candidate would help: contact with a persistent patch can create additional
food that becomes collectible later, alongside immediately available food and visible current-state cues.
This extends inherited approach, collection and depletion responses rather than requiring delayed matching
all at once. Spatial coverage, harvesting mature stock and useful revisits can each be profitable partial
strategies. Keep old worlds as references and evaluate one unchanged genome on reserved combinations.
This is a design preference, not a complete physical contract or authorization to launch a study.

Before calling that interaction informative, check whether active camping, stock-greedy pursuit or patrol
already obtains its benefit; do not manufacture a memory requirement. Separate initial, ordinary-refill and
contact-generated food. A causal probe is worthwhile only if its answer changes the design, and need not
precede exploration. Full mechanism explanation and a hand-built winning body are not admission gates.

One wording refinement is advisable: a bounded comparison can measure accessibility and information payoff,
but cannot generally distinguish a negative result from a representation limit. Replace that promise with
"measure accessibility and information payoff before attributing failure to implementation or representation."
Retaining the full population and ID 1415 while declining an automatic capacity/depth increase is justified.
