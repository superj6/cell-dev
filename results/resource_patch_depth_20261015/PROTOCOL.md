# Deeper acquisition-aware continuation with denser evaluation

The completed population diagnostic found useful variation among the survivors and poor fresh ranks for
both historical reporting endpoints. Pareto selection increased population-average POOL acquisition,
without established population-wide losses on the pursuit diagnostics. This study gives the full Pareto4
population a bounded opportunity to accumulate its strengths with more generations and better measurement.
It tests the combined continuation; it does not isolate a causal advantage of evaluation precision or depth.

## Inheritance and search

Import all 32 ordered Pareto4 survivors, byte-matched to their checkpoint. Native IDs are remapped to 0–31
with the original mapping retained. No favorable founder is filtered in or out. Run eight additional
generations with 32 parents, 224 children, native whole-gene crossover and two point additions. Structural
duplication/deletion remain disabled. Use the already tested Pareto fronts and grouped-coordinate crowding
over patch, POOL and persistent capture means plus ordinary production F. Native tie draws remain unchanged.

Each generation evaluates all 256 candidates on 32 lives, shortlists 64 and evaluates them on 32 more.
Each stage repeats `(patch, POOL, patch, persistent)` eight times: 16/8/8 lives. Survivors therefore have
32/16/16 pooled context counts. Ordinary F, its physical costs and extinction treatment remain unchanged.
The life dynamics remain local and asynchronous. No organism channel, clock, prescribed behavior or
population-responsive world is introduced.

Keep native validation every four generations: zero-based generations 0, 4 and 7, with 16 lives each.
The six native final diagnostics include two half-duration growth lives that reuse existing final seeds.
These observations cannot stop early, substitute a midpoint or choose the reporting endpoint. All eight
generations and their lineage/checkpoint/selection arrays are retained.

## Separate endpoint panel and fresh confirmation

After the complete search, score all 32 final survivors on 32 new shared lives in each of the three training
worlds. Select exactly one reporting genome by the minimum of its capture means divided by the fixed AO
scales `(19.046875, 16.029296875, 5.919921875)`, then descending ordinary F with the original weights
`0.5 patch + 0.25 POOL + 0.25 persistent`, then native ID ascending. Equal panel sizes do not change those
weights. Freeze the selected row, raw genome and all panel data before confirmation. No top-up or repeated
panel is allowed. Native rank zero remains an artifact of search, not the reporting endpoint.

Four fixed roles then share 512 fresh confirmation lives in each of six worlds:

- `AO`: unchanged historical AO, original ID 5336.
- `G8_joint`: G8 local row 20, ID 1795, selected by the completed development-panel rule.
- `Pareto4_joint`: starting-population local row 26, ID 918, selected by that same development-panel rule.
- `depth8_joint`: the new endpoint selected by the separate final-survivor panel above.

The development-selected ancestors are new-study comparison genomes. They do not replace historical G8,
mean4 or Pareto4 reporting endpoints and are not already certified winners. Freshly comparing with a useful
founder helps distinguish added ability from recovery of an existing combination; it cannot prove novelty
over every founding row. The primary contrast is `depth8_joint − Pareto4_joint`. AO and G8 comparisons
measure retention and the earlier buildup on the same lives.

The first five worlds exactly match the completed diagnostic: trained patches, POOL, persistent pursuit,
longer cue gaps, and the now-revealed count-two patch arrangement. The sixth, `reserved_capacity3`, uses
the trained patch physics with four patches, capacity three, placement distances 2.5–4.5 and refill periods
60/180. This is a reserved parameter combination, not a claim of broad unseen-task generalization. Separate
initial-endowment and replenished harvests; its larger initial stock is not evidence of temporal strategy.

## Readouts and interpretation

Report acquisition, initial/refill harvests, physical costs, ordinary F, empty attempts, movement and body
statistics, retaining all guard/extinction rows and their prescribed charges. Use paired whole-life means
and SE, with descriptive three-SE bounds. Report the three old-world 90%-capture margins against the fixed
AO and starting references. A null difference does not establish retention, and a 90% margin permits loss.

Assess cumulative progress from acquisition and retention in one unchanged genome across worlds and the
reserved combination. Economy alone, a larger body, a better training score or a favorable panel maximum
does not establish a new algorithm. Any circuit/memory interpretation needs separate causal evidence.
One continuation is not independent replication of the search strategy. Reconsider a richer accessible
interaction from the completed result; do not automatically begin another sequence of selection tweaks.
