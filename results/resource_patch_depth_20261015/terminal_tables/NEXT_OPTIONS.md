# Directions after the fixed depth result

Prospective suggestions, written without reading this run's outcomes. No next experiment is registered here.

The useful foothold is already concrete: inherited AO pursuit collected replenished food and benefited from
stock-modulated cues before patch evolution. The modulation study identifies a whole-life sensory
contribution. The population panel found useful combinations among survivors and unstable reporting ranks.
That justifies the present search, without making selection refinement the permanent research program.

**1. Let a productive accumulation continue.** Favor one further bounded continuation with unchanged physics,
prices and selection if the fixed descendant adds total/refill acquisition over `Pareto4_joint`, retains useful
older pursuit, and improves the new capacity/radius/refill combination. Gains only against AO, or from lower costs,
are weaker evidence of added acquisition beyond the inherited starting population. The strongest case has one unchanged
genome carrying gains across tasks. Continue
the full population rather than replacing it with whichever reporting genome looks most attractive. This one
trajectory cannot establish reliability or novelty relative to every founder; it also cannot establish a
capacity ceiling if it fails.

**2. Measure a specific contribution to reuse.** Favor a small causal measurement when there is a clear new
food gain but its relationship to inherited sensing remains uncertain, and that answer would change the next
experiment. The most economical first contrast is the evolved gain under native stock modulation versus the
already validated constant-amplitude renderers, applied to the fixed ancestor and descendant. A larger native
advantage in the descendant would support increased dependence on this sensory contribution; it would not
prove a newly learned stock model. Existing lateral/hidden delivery
interventions test signaling dependence, not automatically a shared reusable subcircuit. Development, cell placement,
firing and sensory dose can change under these interventions; a broad freeze is especially hard to interpret.
Retain all outcomes and validity limitations. Mechanistic completeness need not precede new exploration;
these measurements alone do not establish neural memory or prediction.

**3. Add a nearby action-consequence opportunity.** Favor a richer local interaction if another bounded search
mainly improves economy, trades away older acquisition, or shows little new food gain. A promising conceptual
addition is a persistent patch whose local state changes on physical contact and then matures into additional
collectible food. Retain ordinary immediately available food and visible current resource cues: inherited
approach and collection should already earn something, and early encounters can create later opportunities.
This extends existing pursuit and depletion feedback by making an action create productive future stock.
Better spatial coverage, response to mature-stock cues, or useful revisits could each pay before sophisticated
planning appears. No visit order, intermediate behavior bonus, externally imposed phase sequence, or
population-responsive difficulty is needed; the organism remains local and asynchronous.

The important design question is whether this fixed physics leaves several profitable partial strategies.
Permanent camping or current-stock greedy pursuit may already suffice; that is an empirical possibility, not
a reason to demand memory or manufacture a controller gap. Separate initial and contact-generated food, and
compare active alternatives before claiming extra information value. Transfer should combine familiar local
rules in new geometry or timing while testing the same unchanged endpoint on older worlds. The physical contract remains to be designed.

My preference is to reward a genuine acquisition-and-reuse result with more depth. Otherwise, move toward the
accessible action-consequence addition, using a causal probe only to resolve a specific uncertainty. Avoid
another open-ended sequence of mixture, cost and selection tweaks, or requesting more compute before the
completed evidence identifies what additional work would answer.
