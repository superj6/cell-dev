# AO decision: give the lineage more time while testing retention emphasis

Decision after complete AN evidence, 2026-10-15. This document releases no runtime.

The user wants sophisticated evolved algorithms, cumulative capability and generalization, with safe local experimentation. Their latest clarification makes longer evolution or more evaluation an explicit competitor to changing direction; novelty is not required.

AN added longer-gap captures (+0.460938 ±0.110891 over start), and its midpoint already showed that gain while passing both old-task retention diagnostics. Generation4→8 added only +0.035156 ±0.120157 target captures while losing 1.125000 ±0.234506 POOL captures. Nevertheless its fixed weighted F increased about0.108, mostly through economy in moving-target worlds. This is not evidence of a plateau or a precision failure. It suggests the objective can prefer a tradeoff that conflicts with cumulative acquisition.

Alternatives considered:

- Continue the exact allocation longer: useful, cheap relative to new engineering, and eight generations cannot establish saturation. Retain it as one branch.
- Increase selection rollouts: possibly better rankings, but cannot by itself stop a tradeoff rewarded by the true weighted objective. Keep8+8 so the allocation contrast remains interpretable.
- Another harder world: current acquisition is promising but old-task loss makes a further demand less informative now.
- Another price contrast: AM's higher capture value did not jointly help; changing incentives again would obscure this concrete task-allocation question.
- Circuit intervention or operator change: potentially useful, but neither is required before pursuing further acquisition; current findings do not identify either as the bottleneck.
