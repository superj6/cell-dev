The proposed next world is **delayed matching by cue shape**, using only the existing scalar spatial receptor modality and ordinary movement/click actions:

1. At each trial, independently draw an equiprobable narrow/broad sample. Show a stationary Gaussian centered at the body’s position at sample onset, width 0.6 or 1.2, equal peak `lam_s=2`, background 0.05. Sample duration is uniform 15–25 time units, independently of class.
2. Remove the sample completely. Only background remains for an independently drawn uniform 20–40 interval; the organism continues its own dynamics.
3. Show two stationary choices, one of each width and the same peaks. At choice onset, draw a fresh axis, radius uniform 2–4, and random width assignment to opposite ends around the body’s then-current position. Both capture radii are 1, so they cannot overlap. Neither placement nor timing depends on sample class.
4. The first click hitting a choice pays +500 if its width matches the sample, or costs 50 otherwise, and consumes the whole opportunity. Remove both choices and start the next independent sample after 5 units. A choice timeout after 50 units advances likewise without reward. Other clicks cost 10 and never advance the trial. Further clicks in the resolving timestamp group are misses; at most one outcome is paid per trial. Physical maintenance/action costs remain current.

This changes which action is valuable for the **same current choice scene**, according to earlier information. No privileged answer, target identity, reward, phase or time becomes a cell input. Fresh choice placement prevents simply parking at the earlier cue. Independent random streams for class, durations, geometry and side assignment prevent target-slot or schedule correlations. The blank exceeds ordinary pending receptor delivery; persisting cellular traces, chemical state, body shape or motor state are legitimate memory mechanisms, not leakage to erase. They do not by themselves establish a new neural memory algorithm. Pre-positioning and fixed preferences remain measured alternatives; class-conditioned correctness and reward per opportunity include abstentions/timeouts, preventing selective response from inflating success.

The concrete ABI decision would append `task_mode` (0 legacy, 1 delayed match), `sample_min/max`, `blank_min/max`, `choice_T`, and `reset_T`; define trial-state/stream semantics and conditional reward mapping; and append opportunities, correct/wrong choices and timeouts split by sample class. Record unfinished trials at life end. Existing widths, radii and prices supply other parameters.
