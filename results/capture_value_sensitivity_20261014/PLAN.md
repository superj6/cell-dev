confirmation, new evolution run or extension of AC. Use every fixed AC within-world contrast in both backgrounds
and both worlds. Reprice capture income at the declared grid 500 (unchanged), 1000 and 2000 per capture, retaining
all ordinary costs and guard/extinction charges. Do not choose a new genome or report new independent evidence.

receive the corresponding additional income. Form each whole paired vector before computing its SE. Report all

ledger to the organism or physics. Source references: src/life/kernel.c assignments at 1453/2172 and scoring at
1923; src/life/evaluate.py retains price-independent c_live extinction charges. Nevertheless this calculation is
explicitly a repricing of recorded outcomes, not a claim about behavior or populations that would emerge under
changed incentives. No production world, price, genome, selection policy or reported AC primary result is changed.

Inputs are the exact completed AC protocol, frozen summary, paired vectors and terminal clearance. Stop on any
pin mismatch. Preserve the script, grid, all vectors, summary and input/source hashes. No new namespace, genetic
