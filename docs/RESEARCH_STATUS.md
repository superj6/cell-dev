# Project evidence map — completed delayed-matching information measurement

The goal is increasingly sophisticated evolved algorithms, generalization and cumulative capability in the current local asynchronous organism. We have real local computations and repeated capture improvements, but neither larger bodies nor higher fitness establishes algorithmic progress. AO is complete and independently checked: later generations added acquisition in one branch, while task allocation traded older-task retention against further gains elsewhere.

The immediate priority is the authorized [ABI5 delayed shape-matching environment](ABI5_DELAYED_MATCH_PROPOSAL.md): a narrow/broad sample, blank interval and later conditional choice. Local asynchronous cell rules stay unchanged. The validated implementation is integrated; AO's original sources remain archived. Engineering agreement is not evidence of evolved matching. Retention, price tuning and circuit analysis support this work; perfect results on those fronts are not prerequisites. Choose a continuing population explicitly from development evidence, measure earlier skills, and reserve separate cases for generalization.

These are within-experiment paired means ± SE, not addable increments: worlds, seeds and sometimes selection protocols differ. AK directly measured the accumulated forgetting: AI retained only **61.4%** of L92's POOL captures while improving persistent and nearby contexts. [AK](../results/retention_frontier_20261015/REPORT.md). Reusing shared mutation panels across worlds is useful comparative evidence, but not independent evolutionary replication or untouched-task generalization.

AO provides one matched evolutionary pair with 512 fresh confirmation lives per genome/world, not 512 independent searches. All five worlds were development-known. Neither branch adds resolved dark-period captures over AN start, and no new memory or prediction mechanism is demonstrated. Late acquisition in the unchanged branch is real along this observed trajectory; it does not prove a generally superior depth policy. Allocation changes both objective weights and per-context sample counts. The final endpoints remain primary; a favorable midpoint cannot replace them. The unresolved 90% longer-gap margin does not prove a loss exceeding 10%.

The richer-interaction extension has priority. Other directions remain options to reconsider from complete evidence, rather than an automatic sequence:

1. **Stronger information demands.** Delayed matching changes the decision structure so value depends on an earlier cue. AO's gains still do not show improved dark-period acquisition. Measure useful information and conditional choice directly; external controllers establish payoff, not local attainability or an evolved memory algorithm.
2. **More depth or evaluation lives.** AO's unchanged branch adds captures after generation 8; its more-POOL branch later trades POOL captures for economy. Depth deserves consideration when the trajectory supports it, without assuming continued improvement. Favor precision if repeated scoring changes consequential rankings. Rechecking changed 36.5% of parent slots in an older audit, but that does not prove noise is today's main bottleneck. [Pilot review](../results/catch_pilot/design_review.json), [selection audit](../results/selection_noise_20261014/summary.json).
3. **Retention allocation.** AO establishes an allocation tradeoff in this pair: more POOL exposure preserves older captures but gives up later gains elsewhere. More precise optimization alone cannot remove a conflict in what is rewarded. This does not establish an optimal mixture or require perfect retention before richer interaction.
4. **Maintenance or miss incentives.** Favor a targeted price comparison if decomposition identifies a specific damaging pressure. Living costs dominate AL; communication costs are small. AM's higher reward bought more misses and larger bodies without joint capture benefit. Selectively reducing maintenance reverses AN's late mean mixed-F ranking, though the difference remains uncertain. Repricing saved outcomes identifies ranking sensitivity, not what would evolve. [AL cost audit](../results/cost_incentives_20261015/al_posthoc/README.md), [AN cost analysis](../results/an_cost_sensitivity_20261015/NOTE.md).
5. **Operators or causal probes.** Favor operator changes if coadapted combinations repeatedly break; favor a probe when a concrete circuit hypothesis is testable. Whole-gene crossover is a plausible disruption mechanism, not an established bottleneck. Structural-search and copy-knockout nulls do not establish a capacity ceiling. [Operator evidence](../results/capture_value_selection_20261015/discussion/search_operator_notes.md).

Still unproved: robust cumulative retention across independent lineages, broad unseen-task generalization, useful genome expansion, prediction, or a new memory algorithm in this active lineage. A 90% diagnostic permits some loss; a null difference proves neither retention nor absence of an effect.
