# Project evidence map — completed evidence through AN

The goal is increasingly sophisticated evolved algorithms, generalization and cumulative capability in the current local asynchronous organism. We have real local computations and repeated capture improvements, but neither larger bodies nor higher fitness establishes algorithmic progress. The running AO comparison is deliberately excluded from this assessment.

The most specific circuit accounts remain the exploratory BODY-1 pilot: s4 combines neighbour evidence, a sensory fringe and a short reset-dependent latch; s5 combines cooperative sensing with inhibitory feedback. Directional lesions and gain substitutes support parts of these accounts. They are more informative about computation than a score increase, but their original scratch traces were not all retained, as the [mechanism archive explicitly states](../results/catch_pilot/mechanisms.json). The stronger modern, reproducible intervention evidence is narrower: freezing tissue and blocking messages shows added lateral dependence, and D51090 has **+0.794 ±0.207 F** additional hidden-message dependence, almost entirely capture income. This establishes a useful communication contribution, not its algorithm or applicability to the current L92 lineage. [Frozen-tissue evidence](../results/frozen_20261013/summary.json), [hidden-message evidence](../results/hidden_20261014/summary.json).

The active lineage inherits prior genomes. Comparison of the saved float32 genomes shows: L92 equals Y panel row 0; AE selected row 31 of that same panel; AI imports that exact AE genotype; subsequent reference files match their declared parent endpoints. AL onward imports every ordered survivor rather than restarting from rank 0 alone.

These are within-experiment paired estimates, not addable increments: worlds, seeds and sometimes selection protocols differ. AK directly measured the accumulated forgetting: AI retained only **61.4%** of L92's POOL captures while improving persistent and nearby contexts. [AK](../results/retention_frontier_20261015/REPORT.md). Reusing shared mutation panels across worlds is useful comparative evidence, but not independent evolutionary replication or untouched-task generalization.

Five competing directions remain live; none is released by this note:

1. **More depth or evaluation lives.** Favor depth if further generations add confirmed capability without unacceptable losses; favor precision if repeated scoring changes consequential rankings. The pilot sometimes improved after hundreds of generations, while recent searches are short. Rechecking changed 36.5% of parent slots in an older audit, but that does not prove noise is today's main bottleneck. [Pilot review](../results/catch_pilot/design_review.json), [selection audit](../results/selection_noise_20261014/summary.json).
2. **Retention allocation.** Favor it when the actual objective rewards losing old captures. AN's positive late weighted-F estimate (+0.1078±0.0488, unresolved at3SE) alongside POOL loss makes this plausible; more precise optimization alone cannot remove that conflict. AO is testing this, with no result assumed here.
3. **Maintenance or miss incentives.** Favor a targeted price comparison if decomposition identifies a specific damaging pressure. Living costs dominate AL; communication costs are small. AM's higher reward bought more misses and larger bodies without joint capture benefit. Selectively reducing maintenance reverses AN's late mean mixed-F ranking, though the difference remains uncertain. Repricing saved outcomes identifies ranking sensitivity, not what would evolve. [AL cost audit](../results/cost_incentives_20261015/al_posthoc/README.md), [AN cost analysis](../results/an_cost_sensitivity_20261015/NOTE.md).
4. **Stronger information demands.** Favor them if acquisition and retention are secure but success still uses current cues. AN's dark-capture gain is unresolved; bright-period improvement does not demonstrate memory. External controllers establish payoff, not local attainability.
5. **Operators or causal probes.** Favor operator changes if coadapted combinations repeatedly break; favor a probe when a concrete circuit hypothesis is testable. Whole-gene crossover is a plausible disruption mechanism, not an established bottleneck. Structural-search and copy-knockout nulls do not establish a capacity ceiling. [Operator evidence](../results/capture_value_selection_20261015/discussion/search_operator_notes.md).

Still unproved: robust cumulative retention across independent lineages, broad unseen-task generalization, useful genome expansion, prediction, or a new memory algorithm in this active lineage. A 90% diagnostic permits some loss; a null difference proves neither retention nor absence of an effect.
